BDE Lead System: screen map

Each role signs in to its own home screen. Grey boxes are screens. ★ = drawn in this file and clickable. Press Play, then click through.

Start the prototype as

Admin →Upload, allocate, teams, settings Team Lead →Give leads to BDEs, at-risk list BDE →Today, call, outcome, next lead Finance →Admin only: payments, calculators

Every screen has a dark bar at the top to switch role or come back here. Menu items marked "soon" are screens not drawn yet.

Navigation per role

Admin

  • Dashboard ★: lead requests, team comparison
  • Leads: all leads, filters, timeline
  • Upload ★: map columns, duplicates, errors
  • Allocation ★: pool → teams, run history
  • Teams & people ★
  • Attendance: all, corrections to approve
  • Follow-ups: overdue by team
  • Reports + exports
  • Leaderboard
  • Finance ★: own page group
  • Settings ★
  • Audit log

Team Lead

  • Dashboard ★: needs leads, at risk, BDE table
  • Team pool → Distribute ★
  • Leads: team's leads, reassign
  • Follow-ups: pending, overdue per BDE
  • Attendance: team, corrections
  • Payments: record for converted leads
  • Reports (team) + exports
  • Leaderboard

Team switcher at the top when leading more than one team.

BDE

  • Today ★: punch in, next to call, target
  • Lead → Call → Outcome ★ (phone)
  • My leads: by status, search
  • Follow-ups: today, overdue, upcoming
  • Attendance: my month, request correction
  • My performance: day / week / month
  • Leaderboard

Bottom tab bar on phone: Today · Leads · Follow-ups · Me.
